Sample Policy Document (Fictional)

Purpose:
This document describes a fictional compliance policy for a company operating in the EU.

Key points:
1) Minimize collection of personal data to what is strictly necessary.
2) Store customer data securely and restrict access to authorized personnel only.
3) Keep an audit trail of data access and changes.
4) Inform users transparently about what data is collected and why.

Note: This file is included only for demonstration and testing.
